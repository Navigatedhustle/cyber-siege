## The story and its honest comparison

Northwind is fictional. For this scenario its policy does not permit uploading sensitive incident evidence to the selected external AI route or granting it remediation permissions. The cloud-frontier-assistant tile is an original fictional illustration labeled **customer data policy / scripted illustration / no cloud model called**. Its speech is original script text, not a quotation or result from a provider. The request check runs locally; it does not imply that a cloud model received or scanned the preview.

Do not say frontier models cannot help with cybersecurity or local models can bypass safety obligations. Official OpenAI documentation supports authorized defensive investigations and patch validation, acknowledges that some legitimate requests can be flagged, and documents enterprise data controls. Those facts do not imply a universal refusal. Here the differentiator is the approved evidence boundary and a controlled local workflow, not a model-capability or response-time benchmark.

The runbook’s nominal beats are 3/7/12/18/24 seconds, with a pause control. That presentation timing is not a measured recovery benchmark.

### Payment trap

SCOUT spots the invoice warning signs. SHERLOCK explains the clues. SHIELD holds the disputed simulated payment. PATCH restores the normal fictional finance workflow while the questioned payment stays held. SCRIBE queues a human request to verify the vendor on a trusted number before release. The story never invents that a human already made the call.

### Inbox storm

The local model receives 200 fictional messages. The training dataset contains 12 known fakes; that number is a dataset fact, not a validated model-classification accuracy score. The demo quarantines the known fakes and restores simulated inbox workflows. A human identity owner reviews affected accounts and approves any credential change.

### Frozen systems

The squad reviews fictional file/login events. SHIELD stops further fictional records exposure. PATCH applies the verified-backup recovery plan only to city state. SCRIBE queues production patch/restore review for security and service owners. No claim is made that this lab patched a real customer system.

## Value during the scene

1. Bring AI to the evidence: match analysis to the customer’s approved data boundary.
2. Turn clues into understanding: explain sensitive evidence and the next step.
3. Plan capacity for critical work: evaluate dedicated local compute against the workflow’s quotas, connectivity, resilience and support requirements. Real shared local inference and retained peak counters remain visible.
4. Define action scope: tools, permissions and validation govern agent actions.
5. Keep people in control: hand off consequential production changes for approval.

Each card includes a plain-language discovery question. The final Account Action Card reinforces these points and retains its verified HP links and product QR.

An external route blocked by this scenario’s policy cannot perform this workflow under that policy. This is a deployment constraint, not evidence that every frontier model is incapable of cybersecurity. API quotas and temporary overload are documented, as are enterprise capacity options. Do not turn those facts into a prediction of inevitable cloud scarcity or a guarantee of uninterrupted local availability. The sales discovery case is whether dedicated infrastructure fits this customer’s critical workflow, including power, operations, resilience and lifecycle cost.

## Resume, reset and accessibility

New sessions default to the guided story. Old in-progress decision-mode sessions are preserved. Their owners can choose Restart to enter the new story; no one else’s progress is cleared. Reset returns the owner to welcome; Restart begins a fresh guided incident. Both confirm before clearing. A restored active guided session is paused and reruns local analysis when resumed if its prior analysis was interrupted.

Reduce motion and the OS reduced-motion preference disable the floating/route effects. The normal view uses small smooth transforms, no shaking and no flashing red area. Pause freezes demonstration progress and mission-bot movement. The actual model may finish a request while the presentation is paused; counters remain factual.

Background city snapshots are batched once per tick, yielding between groups of rooms so WebSocket controls stay responsive. Direct user actions still commit immediately. A deferred snapshot cannot overwrite a newer owner reset; regression coverage verifies this.

The service remains loopback :8090. Keep the same private SSH/room-hub path; this change does not alter networking, public exposure, drivers, model servers or the original :8080 app.
