# Assets and third-party software

Cyber Siege includes original application source, code-native robot/city/villain art,
fictional evidence, scenario rules, screenshots, local synthesized sound and PDF layouts.
No private visitor records, account details, credentials, model weights or environments
belong in the published source. Historical operational logs are excluded by the
distribution policy. Model and container downloads use the pinned publishers' releases.

The HP logo is an owner-supplied trademark asset; see docs/BRAND_ASSETS.md. HP marks
remain HP property. The supplied HP/Infinity launcher and guide are retained under
vendor/infinity with their existing attribution. No additional license to third-party
marks, model weights, containers or vendor software is granted by this repository.

Python dependencies retain their respective licenses and notices, available in the
installed packages. requirements.lock records the exact tested package versions.
NVIDIA Nemotron weights and serving images, and OpenAI GPT OSS weights and NVIDIA's
vLLM image, are fetched separately from their publishers; review their model cards
and container terms before use. The repository does not redistribute those weights
or images. The optional recorded fallback is a separate release asset, labeled as
recorded playback; it makes no claim of live inference.
